The Swift Refactoring Catalog documents ten code-improvement practices grounded in real Swift projects. It addresses patterns such as protocol-oriented design, safer optional handling, clearer enum modeling, and more expressive function parameters.

The catalog was built from commit-history analysis across ten active open-source repositories and evaluated by Swift developers. The repository includes the dataset, detected refactorings with before-and-after examples, and aggregated survey results. The accompanying paper appears in the *Journal of Software: Evolution and Process*.

This study introduces a practical catalog of ten refactoring practices for Swift. The catalog was developed by analyzing commit histories from ten active open-source Swift repositories and validated through an exploratory survey with Swift developers.

Each practice includes its motivation, mechanics, and before-and-after code examples. The accompanying repository provides the analyzed data, refactoring examples, and aggregated survey results.
